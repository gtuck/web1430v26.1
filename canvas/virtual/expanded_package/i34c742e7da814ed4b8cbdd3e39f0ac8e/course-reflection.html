Course Reflection – Final Project Milestone 5

Due: End of Week 15
Points: 24
Category: Projects (Milestone 5 of 5)

Purpose

The final reflection on your work in this course, submitted alongside the Final Project and graded separately from it.

What to submit

Create projects/final-project/reflection.md, answering the five prompts in the Course Reflection Prompt:

  1. What did you build?
  2. What is the hardest technical problem you solved?
  3. What would you do differently if you started over?
  4. What does "accessible" mean to you now that it didn't at the start of the course?
  5. What are you most proud of in this project?

Write 3–5 sentences for each prompt — roughly 20 sentences in total. Focused, specific, and honest; a single vague sentence per prompt does not meet the standard.

Submit to Canvas: your repository URL, plus a direct link to reflection.md in the text entry.

How this is graded

Against the Final Project – Course Reflection rubric, which is attached to this assignment: depth and specificity, technical honesty, and growth evidence. The full rubric text is in the Course Reflection Prompt.

The strongest reflections name specific code, specific decisions, and specific things that went wrong. "I learned a lot about JavaScript" is the answer to avoid.
