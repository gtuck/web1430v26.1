# Welcome Survey – Introduce Yourself and Your Setup

**Due:** End of Week 0
**Points:** 5
**Category:** Orientation

## Purpose

The welcome survey tells the instructor who is in the course, what experience you bring, and whether anything about your setup or schedule needs attention before the first labs. It also gives you a low-stakes first pass through the weekly submit-in-Canvas habit.

## What to do

1. Open the welcome survey link posted in the Week 00 module and course announcements.
2. Answer every question — it takes about five minutes. There are no wrong answers; honest answers about your experience level and available time help the course support you better.
3. Return to this assignment and submit a short text entry confirming you completed the survey, plus one sentence about what you most want to be able to build by the end of the course.

## Grading

Credit is based on completion: finish the survey and submit the confirmation before the end of Week 0. If the survey link is missing or broken, post in the Help & Questions board and submit your confirmation once it is resolved.
